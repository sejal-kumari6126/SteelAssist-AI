LOCKOUT TAGOUT (LOTO)

Purpose:
Lockout Tagout (LOTO) is a safety procedure used to ensure that machinery is completely shut down before maintenance or servicing.

Objectives:
- Prevent accidental startup
- Protect maintenance personnel
- Eliminate hazardous energy

LOTO Procedure:
1. Inform affected employees.
2. Shut down equipment.
3. Isolate all energy sources.
4. Apply lock and tag.
5. Release stored energy.
6. Verify isolation.
7. Perform maintenance.
8. Remove lock after work completion.

Benefits:
- Prevents injuries
- Improves workplace safety
- Ensures OSHA compliance
